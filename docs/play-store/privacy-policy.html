DataCast Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

DataCast is an app that shares a folder or files from your phone with other devices on the same local Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. The short version: DataCast does not collect, store or send any of your data to us or to anyone else.

Data we collect

None. DataCast has no accounts, no servers run by us, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We never receive your files, your settings or any information about you or your device.

Files you choose to share

DataCast can only read the folder or files you pick with Android's file picker. When you tap Start sharing, the app runs a small web server on your phone that is reachable only from your local network. Another device can see and download those files only after entering the 4-digit PIN shown on your phone. Files travel directly between your phone and that device over your network; they are never uploaded to the internet. Sharing stops when you tap Stop.

The connection on your local network uses plain HTTP, protected by the PIN. Anyone else on the same network who knows the PIN could view the shared files while sharing is on, so share only on networks you trust and keep the PIN private.

Data stored on your phone

DataCast saves its settings on your phone: the device name, the PIN, the network port, and which folder or files you picked. This stays on your device. Android may include it in your own device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. Uninstalling DataCast deletes it.

Network discovery

While sharing, DataCast announces itself on your local network (using mDNS) with its device name so that other DataCast devices and browsers on the same network can list it. This announcement does not leave your local network.

Permissions

DataCast uses network permissions to run the local server and find your Wi-Fi address, a notification permission to show that sharing is active, and a foreground service to keep sharing running in the background until you stop it. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or all of your storage.

Children

DataCast is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
