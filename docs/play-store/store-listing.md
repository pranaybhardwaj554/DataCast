**Full description** (4000 max):

```
DataCast turns your phone into a private file server for your home or office Wi-Fi. Pick a folder or a few files, tap Start sharing, and open the address on your laptop, tablet or another phone. Browse, preview and download, right in the browser. Nothing to install on the other device.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose a folder, or pick individual files to share
• Tap Start sharing and DataCast shows a web address and a QR code
• Open it in any browser on the same Wi-Fi
• Enter the 4-digit PIN shown on your phone, and you're in

WHY DATACAST
• No cloud: files go straight from your phone to the other device over your own network. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Works with any browser: Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebook, iPhone, iPad, Android, smart TVs.
• Fast: transfers run at your Wi-Fi's local speed, not your internet speed.
• Preview photos and videos in a thumbnail grid, then download one file or a whole folder as a ZIP.
• Device list: browsers see every phone sharing with DataCast on the network and pick the one they want.
• PIN protection with lockout after repeated wrong attempts.
• Keeps sharing in the background with a notification and a one-tap Stop button.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
DataCast only reads the folder or files you pick, through Android's own file picker. It can't see anything else on your phone. Sharing stops the moment you tap Stop.

Your phone and the other device need to be on the same Wi-Fi network. Some public or guest Wi-Fi networks block devices from talking to each other; home and most office networks work fine.
```
